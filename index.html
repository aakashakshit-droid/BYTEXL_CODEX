How the Student Success Score works

The Success Score (0 to 100) is a single number that summarises how a student is doing. It combines five indicators, each with a weight, and then subtracts a penalty for backlogs:

  • CGPA (25%): scaled to 100 (CGPA × 10)
  • Attendance (20%): percentage of classes attended
  • LMS completion (15%): share of course modules finished
  • Coding (15%) and Skills (15%): assessment scores out of 100
  • Backlogs: 5 points subtracted for each one

Score = 0.25×(CGPA×10) + 0.20×Attendance + 0.15×LMS + 0.15×Coding + 0.15×Skills − 5×Backlogs, kept between 0 and 100.

Aptitude, engagement and feedback are not part of the score. The risk tier uses the score together with CGPA and backlog rules, shown further down in each report.
